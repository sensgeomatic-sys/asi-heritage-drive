    How we work

    Four clear steps

    1. 1PlanWe agree the area, accuracy and outputs, and arrange flight and site permissions.
    2. 2CaptureDrone imagery, LiDAR, terrestrial scanning and GNSS ground control, collected on site.
    3. 3ProcessOrthomosaics, elevation models, point clouds, 3D models and CAD drawings, checked for accuracy.
    4. 4DeliverFiles handed over, and heritage records stored securely in the Heritage Drive.

    ASI Heritage Drive

    Every survey, drawing and model in one secure place

    Our app keeps Circle records under the AMASR Act: site plans, AutoCAD boundary drawings, notifications and 3D models, organised by Circle and monument. Officers open only what the administrator gives them, and every view and download is logged.

    • View PDFs, AutoCAD and 3D models in the browser
    • Measure distances and areas on drawings
    • See your GPS position on mapped drawings
    • Access by Circle, given by the Super Admin

    About us

    Specialists in heritage and survey data

    Sens Geomatic Pvt. Ltd brings together archaeologists, surveyors, photographers and drone pilots. Every monument has its own materials, history and conservation needs, so we plan each project with care and capture it with precision.

    Government departments, conservation bodies, researchers and private clients use our maps, 3D models and drawings to plan repairs, monitor change and share heritage with the public.
